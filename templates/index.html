Why Upload Chat?

Uploading your actual WhatsApp conversation allows the AI to deeply analyze and learn:

  • How they talk: Exact texting style, sentence patterns, word choices
  • What they talk about: Common topics, interests, and conversation themes
  • Their personality: Emotional expressions, humor style, and unique quirks
  • Language patterns: Code-switching, slang, emojis they actually use

Result: Much more accurate and authentic responses! Without chat upload, the bot will only use your manual persona description.

Your Privacy is Protected

  • Local Processing: Your chat is parsed directly on this server
  • No External Storage: Files are deleted immediately after processing
  • Secure API: Your Gemini API key is stored locally and encrypted
  • Private Data: Conversation pairs stay on your device only

We never upload, share, or store your conversations externally. Everything happens locally.

help_outline How to export WhatsApp chat?

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to the chat you want to export
  2. Tap the three dots (menu) → More → Export chat
  3. Choose "Without Media"
  4. Save the .txt file and upload it here
